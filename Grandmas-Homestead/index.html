Grandma's Farming Field Guide
Farming Fundamentals
  1. Till the Soil: Equip the Hoe (1) and click on grass or dirt.
  2. Plant Seeds: Select any seed packet (Turnip, Carrot, Strawberry, etc.) and click tilled soil.
  3. Water Daily: Equip your Watering Can (2). Seeds only grow on days they are watered!
  4. Refill Water: Click on the stone well or the pond on the east side to fill your can to 20/20.
  5. Clear Debris: Use Scythe (3) for weeds, Axe (4) for wood logs, and Pickaxe (5) for stone boulders.
  6. Harvest & Sell: When crops shimmer with a golden sparkle, click them to harvest. Sell individual items or stacks at the Village Shop or Shipping Bin for Gold!
  7. Go Fishing: Equip the Fishing Rod by Lake Hazel (east farm dock) or Oakhaven Canal! Press Space / Click / USE when you see ! BITE !, then tap inside the green bar to catch 6 fish species!
  8. Forage & Cook: Gather wild flowers, mushrooms, berries, and honey around the farm, then cook hearty recipes at the Kitchen Table inside Grandma's Cottage!
