# Grandma's Homestead - Farming Simulator & Oakhaven City Expansion

A cozy, Stardew Valley-inspired HTML5 farming simulator featuring Grandma Hazel's countryside homestead, the bustling explorable town of Oakhaven City, full inventory backpack system, pixel art aesthetic, interactive tutorial quests, full crop cultivation, dynamic day/night cycles, peaceful acoustic music, and community trade.

---

## Story & Premise

You inherit Grandma Hazel's cherished countryside farm. Arriving at the old homestead, you meet Grandma Hazel relaxing on the wooden porch in her rocking chair with a hot cup of chamomile tea. The farm has overgrown weeds, boulders, and untilled plots—waiting for your green thumb to bring it back to vibrant life!

Follow the main cobblestone road straight south from Grandma's house past the open countryside road gates to enter **Oakhaven City**, a charming town where you can trade, accept community notice board quests, upgrade your tools, and make friends with local townspeople and pets!

---

## Key Features

### 1. Full Player Backpack & Inventory System (`I` Hotkey & UI Button)
- **Unlimited Item Overview**: Press **`I`** or click the **Inventory** button to open your complete backpack modal, displaying every tool, seed, crop, food, and material in your possession.
- **Hotbar Slot Assignment (1 – 8)**: Click **"Equip to Slot"** on any item to place it in your active hotbar slot or swap it with another item.
- **Category Filter Tabs**: Sort your inventory by **All Items**, **Tools**, **Seeds & Crops**, **Food & Energy**, or **Materials**.
- **Instant Food Consumption**: Eat energy-restoring foods directly from the inventory window (Grandma's Pie, Baguettes, Cinnamon Buns, Tarts, Wild Honey) to keep your stamina full without wasting field time.
- **Hotbar Mini-Preview**: See at a glance which 8 items are loaded into your quick-access hotbar slots.

### 2. Farming & Homestead Mechanics
- **Tilling**: Use your Hoe to till fertile soil plots (grass only; cobblestone paths are protected).
- **Planting**: Sow Turnip, Carrot, Strawberry, Blueberry, Tomato, Golden Corn, Sweet Melon, Giant Pumpkin, and rare Starfruit seeds.
- **Watering**: Water crops daily with your Watering Can. Crops only advance in growth on days they receive water!
- **Refilling**: Click on Grandma's stone well, Lake Hazel, or Oakhaven City's central fountain to refill your watering can.
- **Fertilizer**: Apply Speed-Gro Fertilizer to cut crop growing time in half.
- **Automated Sprinklers**: Buy and install sprinklers that auto-water adjacent crops every morning!
- **Harvesting**: Golden harvest shimmer indicates when crops are ready. Pick them to add to your inventory!

### 3. Oakhaven City Explorable World
- **Seamless Southbound Road Exit**:
  - The main north-south cobblestone road from Grandma's house goes straight down to the bottom of the map through a wide gate with a signpost (**"OAKHAVEN CITY v v"**).
  - Simply walk south off the bottom of the farm to transition directly into Oakhaven City.
  - In Oakhaven, walk north through the entrance archway or signpost (**"TO GRANDMA'S FARM ^ ^"**) to return home.
  - A convenient top-bar fast travel button (**"Visit City" / "Return to Farm"**) is also available at any time.
- **Central Cobblestone Plaza & Stone Fountain**:
  - An interactive multi-tier stone fountain bubbling with fresh spring water—click it to refill your watering can.
  - Comfortable park benches and street lamps that glow warmly at dusk and night.
- **Community Notice Board**:
  - View requests from townspeople (e.g. banquet vegetables, bakery fruits, bench restoration wood, masonry stone).
  - Turn in farm produce and materials directly for gold rewards.
- **4 City Buildings & Shops**:
  - **General Store**: Stock up on standard and rare seeds (including Blueberry, Melon, and Starfruit).
  - **Hazelwood Bakery & Café**: Purchase and eat Nora's fresh French Baguettes, Cinnamon Rolls, Hazelberry Fruit Tarts, and Hot Cocoa to instantly restore stamina.
  - **Garth's Blacksmith Forge**: Reinforce tools using gold and quarry stone:
    - *Copper Hoe*: Cuts tilling energy cost by 50%.
    - *Steel Forester Axe*: Chops trees in 2 hits instead of 3.
    - *Steel Prospector Pickaxe*: Shatters boulders in a single strike.
    - *Deluxe Brass Can*: Expands water capacity to 40 units.
  - **City Hall**: The administrative heart of Oakhaven where Mayor Clara organizes town festivals.
- **6 Lively City NPCs & Town Mascot**:
  - **Mayor Clara**: Welcomes you to Oakhaven and shares stories about your grandfather's legacy.
  - **Nora the Baker**: Discusses her artisanal sourdough and wild berry tarts.
  - **Garth Ironhand**: Offers tool forging and metallurgical wisdom.
  - **Barnaby the Fisherman**: Angling along the canal bridge with advice on river fishing and clean spring water.
  - **Penny the Florist**: Sells jars of Wildflower Honey and teaches flower botanicals.
  - **Jasper the Town Pup**: An enthusiastic golden retriever who gives friendly tail wags and a +5 Energy boost whenever you pet him!

### 4. Interactive Tutorial & Grandma's Quests
- Live HUD banner tracking current homestead milestones.
- Interactive Questbook modal with rewards for each step:
  1. *Grandma's Welcome*: Greet Grandma Hazel on the porch (Rewards Gold + Turnip Seeds).
  2. *Prepare the Earth*: Till 3 soil patches (Rewards Gold + Carrot Seeds).
  3. *Sow Your First Seeds*: Plant seeds on tilled soil (Rewards Gold + Fertilizer).
  4. *Nourish the Sprouts*: Water your crops (Rewards Gold + Grandma's Apple Pie).
  5. *Farm Beautification*: Clear weeds, trees, and rocks (Rewards Wood, Stone, and Gold).
  6. *Visit the Countryside Market*: Explore the seed and supply shop (Rewards Gold).
  7. *Rest & Harvest Tomorrow*: On Day 1, talk to Grandma Hazel on the porch before turning in for bed!

### 5. Countryside Merchant Shop & Shipping Bin
- **Buy Tab**: Complete seed catalogue (Turnips, Carrots, Strawberries, Blueberries, Tomatoes, Corn, Melons, Pumpkins, Starfruits), Speed-Gro Fertilizer, Sprinklers, Grandma's Pies.
- **Sell Tab**: Sell harvested vegetables, fruit, wood, and stone for Gold. Includes a one-click **"Sell All Produce"** button.
- Roadside market stall and rustic wooden shipping bin on the farm map.

### 6. Atmosphere & Audiovisuals
- Procedural pixel art (cottage with chimney smoke, Grandma in rocking chair, rippling lake, canal bridge, plaza fountain, town buildings, wagging dog).
- Dynamic Time & Weather: Sunrise, bright afternoon, golden sunset, cozy night lighting, and gentle rain days (which auto-water all crops!).
- Procedural Web Audio API sound effects (hoe digging, water splashes, seed plucking, coin jingles, rooster calls, and calming acoustic guitar chord progressions).
- Clean vector SVG UI icons (strictly zero raw emojis).
- Custom stylized parchment and wood modal dialogs (no native browser alerts).

## Controls

| Key / Action | Function |
| :--- | :--- |
| **W, A, S, D** or **Arrow Keys** | Walk around (S moves down without opening the shop) |
| **I** | **Open / Close Full Backpack & Inventory** |
| **B** or **P** | Open Countryside Merchant Shop / General Store |
| **Left Click / Tap** | Use equipped tool / plant seeds / interact / click to walk |
| **Space** or **E** | Use tool / interact with facing tile |
| **1 – 8** | Select Hotbar item |
| **Walk South off Farm / Road Gate** | **Travel to Oakhaven City** |
| **Walk North off City / Gate** | **Return to Grandma's Farm** |
| **Well / Pond / Lake / Fountain** | Refill Watering Can |
| **Travel Button** | Fast travel between Grandma's Farm and Oakhaven City |
| **Notice Board Click** | Open Oakhaven Community Bulletin |
| **Cottage Door / Rest Button** | Sleep and advance to next morning (On Day 1, talk to Grandma first!) |
| **Tutorial Button / T** | Open Grandma's Questbook and tutorial tracker |
| **Guide Button** | Open Crop Almanac & profit table |
| **Audio Button** | Toggle music & sound effects |

## How to Play

Open `index.html` in any modern web browser or run `http://localhost:8085/`. No installation or external dependencies required!
