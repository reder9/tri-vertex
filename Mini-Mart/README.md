# Pixel Mini Mart - Tycoon Simulator

A fun, fast-paced casual market tycoon simulator with a vibrant cartoon/pixel aesthetic!

## Core Gameplay
1. **Harvest & Produce**:
   - **Tomato Garden**: Walk over ripe tomato plants to harvest juicy tomatoes.
   - **Chicken Coop**: Collect fresh eggs from clucking chickens.
   - **Apple Orchard**: Pick sweet green and red apples from the tree.
   - **Dairy Cow Pen**: Gather bottled fresh milk from grazing dairy cows.
   - **Bakery Oven**: Bake warm loaves of bread.
2. **Stack & Restock**:
   - Carry items in a wobbly, physics-based vertical stack on your back!
   - Walk near display shelves to automatically restock them for customers.
3. **Checkout & Revenue**:
   - Customers arrive, browse aisles, fill shopping baskets, and line up at the register.
   - Stand behind the checkout counter to scan items (*beep beep!*) and collect stacks of cash.
4. **Expand & Hire**:
   - Step onto glowing unlock pads to spend earnings:
     - Expand new aisles (Eggs, Milk, Apples, Bakery).
     - **Hire Assistant Stocker**: Runs around restocking shelves automatically.
     - **Hire Cashier**: Scans customer groceries at the counter automatically.
     - **Speed & Capacity Upgrades**: Increase sprint speed and carry stack capacity.

## Controls
- **PC**:
  - `WASD` / `Arrow Keys`: Move player
  - `Spacebar`: Turbo Sprint
  - `E`: Open Store Upgrades
  - `P` / `Escape`: Pause Game
- **Xbox Controller**:
  - `Left Stick` / `D-Pad`: Analog movement with radial deadzone
  - `Button A`: Turbo Sprint
  - `Button X` / `Button Y`: Open Store Upgrades
  - `Start`: Pause Game
  - Haptic feedback when collecting cash or unlocking new aisles
- **Mobile & Tablet**:
  - Dynamic floating virtual joystick (touch & drag anywhere)
  - On-screen `TURBO` sprint button
  - Touch buttons for pause and upgrades
