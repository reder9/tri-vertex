# Leyten's Games Collection

A multi-game repository and arcade suite featuring:

## Games in this Collection

### 1. [Tri-Vertex 2.0](./Tri-Vertex/)
- **Genre**: Cosmic Arena Combat / Twin-Stick Starfighter Shooter
- **Controls**: Xbox Controller (full twin-stick 360° spin, trigger fire, haptics), Keyboard & Mouse, and Mobile/Tablet Touch Joystick
- **Features**: 3200 x 2400 cosmic arena, dynamic camera follow, 4 melee weapons, 5 ranged weapons, Super Nova ultimate, parry/deflect system, 6 upgrade tiers.
- **Entry**: [`Tri-Vertex/index.html`](./Tri-Vertex/index.html)

### 2. [Pixel Mini Mart](./Mini-Mart/)
- **Genre**: Cartoon / Pixel Market Tycoon Simulator
- **Controls**: Xbox Controller (left-stick radial navigation, Button A turbo sprint, Button X/Y upgrades, Start pause), Keyboard (WASD/Arrows, Space turbo, E upgrades), and Mobile Virtual Touch Joystick
- **Features**: Production farm plots (Tomatoes, Egg Coop, Apple Orchard, Dairy Cow Milk, Bakery Oven), display shelves, checkout counter with scanning queue, physics item stack on player's back, hireable staff helpers and cashiers, store expansion unlock zones.
- **Entry**: [`Mini-Mart/index.html`](./Mini-Mart/index.html)

### 3. [Grandma's Homestead](./Grandmas-Homestead/)
- **Genre**: Cozy Farming Simulator & Oakhaven City Expansion
- **Controls**: Mouse, Touch, Keyboard Hotkeys (`I` for full backpack inventory)
- **Features**: Crop cultivation, watering and fertilizer, stamina and food system, southern road connection to Oakhaven City, central stone fountain, notice board quests, 4 city shops (General Store, Hazelwood Bakery, Blacksmith Forge).
- **Entry**: [`Grandmas-Homestead/index.html`](./Grandmas-Homestead/index.html)
